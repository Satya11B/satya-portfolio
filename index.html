Spot Kick

Twelve Yards is a penalty shootout game you play in your browser. You take 5 shots at the goalie, then you are the goalie for 5 shots. If it's tied, it goes to sudden death until somebody wins. You pick left, center, or right, and so does the computer, so you have to guess what it will do. I made it with help from YouTube videos and some AI chatbots, and I tested it a lot on my dad's laptop.

Super Over

Super Over is a cricket game you play in your browser. First you bat for 6 balls and try to score as many runs as you can with only 2 wickets. Then you swap over and bowl 6 balls to defend your score. If the scores are the same, it goes to another Super Over until someone wins. I learned how to build it from YouTube, came up with the ideas myself, and used some AI models to help me write the code and fix my mistakes.
